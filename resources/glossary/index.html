Glossary

Every term on a spec sheet.

Definitions used on this site and in the DAYIN catalogue.

Segment
The pattern of holes on a soft-tip target face. DAYIN builds four: PRO, STD, JMB and NEO.
STD
Novice-size segment: 13.5″ face at 2.37 m with an enlarged bullseye and wider double and triple rings.
PRO
Traditional-size segment: 13.5″ face at 2.37 m with the smallest bull and rings of the four.
JMB
Large-face segment: 15.5″ face at 2.44 m with six rows on the triple and double rings.
NEO
Commercial stand-up segment: 15.5″ face at 2.44 m, as used on commercial stand-up machines.
Variation
A rule option within a game, such as Double In/Out in 01 games.
Cricket readout
A display that shows the marks a player has on the cricket numbers.
OX
Cricket readouts built into the door panel at a larger size.
Flexible-light LCD (S)
An LCD with illuminated digits.
Backlit LCD (L)
An LCD lit from behind.
LED seven-segment (E)
A display built from seven-segment LED digits; it cannot render full-LCD layouts.
Full-LCD layout
A display code (82, 89, 92, 95, 99) with its own screen size and layout.
Miss detect
A feature that registers darts that miss the scoring area.
OEM
Original equipment manufacturing: an existing model built under your brand.
ODM
Original design manufacturing: a new product designed and built by the manufacturer for your brand.
Private label
A product sold under the buyer’s brand.
20′ / 40′ container load
How many units of a model fit in a 20-foot or 40-foot container, as listed on each model page.
