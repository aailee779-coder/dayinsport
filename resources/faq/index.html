FAQ

Questions buyers ask.

Short answers, from the DAYIN catalogue. Anything not covered here is answered in your quotation.

What does DAYIN make?

Electronic dartboards (13.5″ and 15.5″), Bluetooth dartboards, cabinet dartboards, no-power soft-tip, silent and magnetic boards, and accessories, as ODM and OEM.

How many models are in the catalogue?

77 models in the 2026 catalogue, in Entry, Advanced, Professional, Tournament Size, Connected, Traditional and Accessories series.

How fast is a quotation?

Within 72 hours: proposal, custom design recommendations and a detailed quote in one reply.

How long do samples take?

7–14 days.

How long is mass production?

25–60 days on in-house lines.

What is the MOQ?

It depends on the model and the customisation, and is confirmed in your quotation.

What can be customised?

Target segment (PRO, STD, JMB, NEO), face, housing and door colours, door panel profile, display type and layout, and branding. ODM projects can change hardware, firmware and the app.

Which certifications do you have?

CE, FCC and RoHS, among other international safety approvals. Documents are available on request.

Where are your factories?

Dongguan, China (since 2004) and Bắc Ninh, Vietnam (since 2024).

Can you produce outside China?

Yes. The Bắc Ninh plant gives clients who need a non-China origin a second manufacturing base.

What warranty do you offer?

A 12-month warranty, technical support and a spare-parts supply guarantee.

Do your Bluetooth boards have an app?

Yes. SDBplay, developed and maintained by DAYIN, with online play, game logs, friends, awards and an online store.

How many games do the boards have?

The catalogue lists 41 games with 356 variations; each model page shows the count for that model.

What do STD, PRO, JMB and NEO mean?

They are the four target segments, differing in face size, throw distance and hole rows. See the segment guide.

How do I read a model name like WDH2-54S?

54 is the display layout (1 current score + 4 player scores + 4 cricket readouts) and S is the display type (flexible-light LCD). See the display code guide.

How do I contact DAYIN?

Email sport@madeindayin.com or send a quote request.
