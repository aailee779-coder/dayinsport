Correct throwing posture: grip, stance, release

Hold the dart lightly between your index finger and thumb, keep it balanced, stand with about 90% of your weight on your front foot, and release smoothly using your elbow as the pivot, like throwing a paper plane. These are the posture tips printed in the DAYIN catalogue; they suit every DAYIN dartboard.

Grip

Hold the dart correctly! Keep the dart balanced, don't grip too hard, hold it lightly. Place the dart on your index finger and throw. Hold the dart with your index finger and thumb.

Incorrect grip: gripping too tightly with the fingers.

Stance

Find your center of gravity. Stand with proper posture! Balance evenly on both sides. Imagine a wooden pole running vertically through your body from head to toe, supporting your entire stance. Place 90% of your body weight on your front foot and 10% on your back foot for the optimal stance.

Incorrect stance: weight leaning too far back or body tilted.

Throwing

Release smoothly! Use your elbow as a pivot point and swing your forearm forward, releasing the dart gently like throwing a paper airplane. Keep your eye on the target and stay focused.
