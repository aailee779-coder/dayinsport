Dartboard target types: STD vs PRO vs JMB vs NEO

STD, PRO, JMB and NEO are the four target segments DAYIN builds. PRO and STD are 13.5″ faces thrown from 2.37 m; JMB and NEO are 15.5″ faces thrown from 2.44 m. They differ in the number of hole rows on the bull and the scoring rings: more rows mean a larger scoring area and an easier hit. Any of the four can be specified on a private label board.

PRO

13.5" face at 2.37 m, the same size as a traditional bristle board. The smallest bullseye and rings of the four - built for players who want precision to count.

STD

The same 13.5" face at 2.37 m, but with an enlarged bullseye and wider double and triple rings so beginners still score. Used on 63 of the 82 models in this catalogue.

JMB

15.5" face at 2.44 m with six rows on both the triple and double rings - the widest scoring rings of the four. A full-size face that is still forgiving.

NEO

The same segment used on commercial stand-up machines: 15.5" face at 2.44 m. What you practise on at home behaves like the one in the venue.

Which one should you choose?

Choose STD for a first home dartboard: the enlarged bull and wider rings let beginners score. Choose PRO for players who want a traditional bristle-board size where precision counts. Choose JMB for a full-size 15.5″ face that is still forgiving, and NEO when home practice should behave like a commercial stand-up machine.
