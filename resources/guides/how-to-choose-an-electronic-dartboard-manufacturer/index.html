How to choose an electronic dartboard manufacturer

Choose an electronic dartboard manufacturer that owns the steps that decide your product: moulds, injection moulding, electronics and the app. Then check certifications for your market, sample and production lead times, traceability, and whether a non-China origin is available. Here is the checklist, and how DAYIN’s OEM & ODM service answers it.

The checklist

Ask for certificate documents per model (e.g. CE for Europe, FCC for the US, RoHS).		CE, FCC and RoHS named; documents on request.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

OEM or ODM?

OEM when an existing model fits and you need your brand on it. ODM when you need a product that does not exist yet.

What should I send with an RFQ?

Models or a description, quantity, target market and the customisation you need.
