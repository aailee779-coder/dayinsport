FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who develops the firmware and the app?

DAYIN’s own team. Firmware, app and scoring software are developed in Taipei, and the Connected-series app is maintained in-house, not by a third party.

Do you make the moulds yourselves?

Yes. DAYIN has its own mould workshop and the group has 30 years of mould and injection-moulding experience, so samples do not wait for an outside vendor.

Have you developed products other than dartboards?

Yes. Other development cases include a golf stroke counter, a basketball scorer, a table-tennis scorer, a bicycle computer and dart accessories.
