FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is the difference between OEM and ODM?

OEM: you take an existing DAYIN model and we build it with your brand, colours, doors, display and packaging. ODM: we define and develop a new product with you, from industrial design and moulds to hardware, firmware and app.

How fast do I get a quotation?

Proposal, customisation advice and a detailed quote come back in one reply within 72 hours, according to the DAYIN service process.

What is the minimum order quantity?

It depends on the model and on how much is customised. It is confirmed in your quotation.
