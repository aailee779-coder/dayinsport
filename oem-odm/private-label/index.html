The four segments

More rows, easier hits.

Four segment options - PRO, STD, JMB and NEO - differing in target face size and in the number of hole rows on the face. More rows mean a larger scoring area and an easier hit. Any of the four can be specified.

Segment guide

PRO

13.5" face at 2.37 m, the same size as a traditional bristle board. The smallest bullseye and rings of the four - built for players who want precision to count.

STD

The same 13.5" face at 2.37 m, but with an enlarged bullseye and wider double and triple rings so beginners still score. Used on 63 of the 82 models in this catalogue.

JMB

15.5" face at 2.44 m with six rows on both the triple and double rings - the widest scoring rings of the four. A full-size face that is still forgiving.

NEO

The same segment used on commercial stand-up machines: 15.5" face at 2.44 m. What you practise on at home behaves like the one in the venue.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can I put my logo on the board and the box?

Yes. Private label covers the board, the face and housing colours, the door panels and the packaging.

Which options can be combined?

Any of the four segments can be specified. Face, housing and door colours can be matched to your palette. Displays combine a type letter with a layout code, except that LED seven-segment displays cannot render full-LCD layouts.

What happens after I send a configuration?

You receive a proposal, customisation advice and a detailed quote in one reply within 72 hours. Samples follow in 7–14 days.
