Process & lead times

From brief to container, in six steps.

DAYIN Dartboard Division has established a full-process service system from needs consultation to after-sales service, ensuring professional support at every stage for our clients.

  1. Needs consultation

    Understanding the application, style, quantity, and customization requirements; accurately grasping client needs.

  2. Solution &amp; quotation

    Providing product proposals, custom design recommendations, and detailed quotations based on requirements with rapid response.

    72 hours

  3. Sample confirmation

    Producing samples quickly for physical confirmation before mass production, ensuring quality meets expectations.

    7-14 days

  4. Mass production

    Scheduling production on in-house lines with strict quality control and on-time delivery.

    25-60 days

  5. Logistics &amp; shipping

    Integrated supply chain, multi-modal transport, global reach.

  6. After-sales service

    12-month warranty + technical support + spare parts supply guarantee.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does a quotation take?

Proposal, custom design recommendations and a detailed quotation within 72 hours.

How long do samples take?

7–14 days for samples you can confirm physically before mass production.

How long is mass production?

25–60 days on in-house lines, depending on the model, the customisation and the quantity.

What is the minimum order quantity?

It depends on the model and the customisation, and is confirmed in your quotation.

What warranty do you give?

A 12-month warranty, technical support and a spare-parts supply guarantee.
