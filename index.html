About DAYIN

One team, from mould to shelf.

DAYIN has made electronic dartboards in Chang’an, Dongguan since 2004. Moulds, injection, electronics, assembly and the app all stay in-house, so your project has one owner from first sketch to final carton.

  • Own mould workshop. Tooling is not subcontracted.
  • Own software team in Taipei for firmware and the SDBplay app.
  • Two origins. Dongguan, China and Bắc Ninh, Vietnam.

OEM & ODM

Your board, built in four steps.

One project team takes your idea through design, tooling, production and packing.

How OEM & ODM works

  1. Design

    Face, housing, display and firmware defined with your team.

  2. Mould

    Tooling made in our own mould workshop.

  3. Injection

    Housings and faces moulded on our own machines.

  4. Assembly & packing

    Tested, packed in your carton, ready to ship.
