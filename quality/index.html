FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which certifications do your dartboards have?

CE, FCC and RoHS, among other international safety approvals named in the DAYIN catalogue. Certificate documents are available on request.

How is scoring accuracy checked?

High-precision sensors register each hit in milliseconds, and the target face is built to take hundreds of thousands of impacts.

Can a fault be traced?

Yes. Production is traceable from mould to finished unit, so when something goes wrong you can find the step it came from.
