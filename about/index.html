About DAYIN

Built for the game. Built for your brand.

DAYIN was founded in 2004 in Chang'an, Dongguan, as part of the JINWEI Group. Product development, mold making, injection molding, secondary processing and electronic assembly all happen inside our own plants - clients do not split the work across vendors, and do not manage the gaps between them.

The company

One roof since 2004.

Backed by the group's thirty years of mold experience and 100+ patents, our work spans cleaning appliances, lifestyle products and sporting goods. In 2024 we opened a plant in Bac Ninh, Vietnam, giving clients who need a non-China origin a second manufacturing base.

We keep product definition, hardware and software development, mold making, injection molding and electronic assembly under one roof, delivering complete ODM/OEM service from concept to mass production - home electronic dartboards, commercial dart machines, children's dartboards, magnetic dartboards, silent dartboards, Bluetooth dartboards and accessories. Backed by the group's 30 years of mold and injection molding experience and full in-house process control, we are a long-term partner to a number of international brands.

The DAYIN catalogue also names DARTMAN as DAYIN’s own brand, selling in Japan and Taiwan. [To confirm with DAYIN before launch.]
