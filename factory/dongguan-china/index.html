DAYIN Dongguan, China · since 2004

Where DAYIN dartboards have been made since 2004.

DAYIN was founded in 2004 in Chang’an, Dongguan, as part of the JINWEI Group. Tooling and precision injection moulding are in Chang’an; electronic dartboard assembly and finished goods are in Hongmei, Dongguan.
