Questions

Bluetooth dartboards, answered.

Who develops the app?

The SDBplay app is developed and maintained by DAYIN’s own team, so changes are made when they are needed and are not gated by a third party.

What can players do in the app?

Online play with sharing links, game logs and statistics, friends, awards, an online store for backgrounds and sound packs, multiple sub-accounts and quick play without login.
