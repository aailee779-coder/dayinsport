FAQ

15.5″ tournament-size dartboards, answered.

What is the difference between JMB and NEO faces?

Both are 15.5″ faces at a 2.44 m throw. JMB has six rows on the triple and double rings, the widest scoring rings of the four segments. NEO is the segment used on commercial stand-up machines.

Can a wooden cabinet be added?

Yes. A wooden cabinet can be added where storage or a more finished look is wanted.
