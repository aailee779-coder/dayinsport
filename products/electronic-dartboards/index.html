FAQ

Electronic dartboards, 13.5″, answered.

Which segment do these boards use?

Most use the STD segment: a 13.5″ face with an enlarged bullseye and wider double and triple rings, so beginners still score. PRO, JMB and NEO faces can be specified.

Can the face colours be matched to my brand?

Yes. Target face, housing and door colours can be matched to your brand palette rather than picked from a stock range.
