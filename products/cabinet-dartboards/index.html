FAQ

Cabinet dartboards with doors, answered.

Which door panels are available?

Plastic or wooden panels in the profiles shown in the catalogue (CB, CT, DB, GB, MD, ZB, VB and W). The door profile can be specified to your brand.

What does “OX” in a model code mean?

OX means the cricket readouts are built into the door panel at a larger size. They work exactly like on-body readouts; only the physical size differs.
