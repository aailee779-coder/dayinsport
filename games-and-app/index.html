Games & app

41 games. 356 variations.

Players do not get bored. Classics for competition, party games for mixed company, and an app that puts an opponent in another city. Game counts vary by model; each model page lists its own.

1 game
  • 01 GamesThe classic pub countdown. Start at 301, subtract every dart, first to exactly zero wins. Switch on Double In/Out and the same board runs a real match.

Cricket games

7 games
  • CricketThe tournament standard. Only 15-20 and the bull are live: close them out while staying ahead on points. It rewards tactics, not strength.
  • Scram (2 Players)Two rounds with the roles reversed: one player closes numbers while the other scores off whatever is still open, then you swap. The most head-to-head of the two-player games.
  • Cut Throat CricketEvery point you score is dumped on your opponents, and the lowest total wins. Digging holes for other people - the more players, the better it gets.
  • English Cricket (2 Players)One player needs nine bulls; the other must beat 40 points every turn just to score. Two completely different jobs on the same board.
  • Random CricketThe computer picks six random numbers plus the bull, so no two games use the same targets. Practised routines count for nothing here.
  • Low CricketPlayed on 1-6 and the bull. Smaller targets, lower scores, a completely different rhythm - and useful practice on the numbers most players ignore.
  • Advanced CricketOnly doubles and triples count, shrinking the scoring area to two narrow rings. The step up for players who have outgrown standard Cricket.

Practice games

8 games
  • ShooterEach round the display flashes a target; hit it and you score. One sentence of rules, so children and first-time throwers can join straight away.
  • Trap ShootThree targets, fifteen seconds, like shooting clays. Once the countdown starts, the people watching get more nervous than the thrower.
  • Count UpFirst to the target total - 400, 500, whatever you set - wins. The simplest game on the board, ideal as a warm-up or for teaching a newcomer.
  • High ScoreNine darts, highest total wins. A three-minute game - the one to run when there is a queue waiting for the board.
  • Round the ClockWork from 1 up to 20 in order, no skipping. Start at 5, 10 or 15, or restrict scoring to doubles or triples only - the difficulty is yours to set.
  • Shanghai1 through 20, three darts at each, highest total wins. Start at 5, 10 or 15 - or step up to Super Shanghai, where the doubles and triples are called for you.
  • Nine Dart CenturyReach 100 in nine darts - closest wins, over is a bust. Easy to explain, and much harder to finish than it sounds.
  • Gold HuntEvery 50 points banks a nugget and every 100 banks two; first to the set number wins. A visible goal and a fast pace.

Party games

10 games
  • Big SixWhatever you hit becomes your opponent's next target. Set them a triple 20, watch a life disappear - last player standing wins.
  • OversEvery turn has to beat your own last turn, so the bar keeps rising. Fall short and you lose a life - the pressure builds on its own.
  • UndersOvers in reverse: each turn must score less than the one before. A miss or a bounce-out adds 60 penalty points, so control beats power.
  • KillerHit the double of your own number to become a Killer, then take lives off everyone else. Put a crowd on it and alliances form within minutes.
  • Double DownScore and you add; miss for a whole turn and your total is halved. One bad round costs half of everything you have built.
  • Double Down 41Double Down reversed, with one extra round at the end: make exactly 41 with three darts. That final round is where the game is won or lost.
  • All FivesEach three-dart turn must divide by five, and every five scores a point. Here the quick mental arithmetic beats the steady hand.
  • BowlingTen frames: pick your lane, then build the pin count with two darts. A perfect game is 200, and the scoring has real depth to study.
  • Shove a PennyThree points clears each number, and anything over spills onto the next player. Part relay race, part passing the parcel.
  • Tic-Tac-Toe (2 Players)Three hits on a number claims that square, and a line of three wins. Rules that need no explaining anywhere in the world.

Sports games

6 games
  • Car RallyYou build the track yourself - at least twenty segments, obstacles wherever you want them. First to finish wins, and no two races are alike.
  • Horseshoes (2 Players)The whole game runs on just two numbers, 20 and 3, one each. Only the higher score in a round counts, and first to 15 wins.
  • GolfSegments 1 to 18 are the holes; sink each one in as few darts as you can. A triple on the first dart is an eagle - any golfer gets it immediately.
  • FootballAdvance from your own number, through the bull, to the number opposite - eleven segments in strict order, like driving the length of the field.
  • BaseballNine innings, and three darts are three batters: a single takes first base, a double takes second, a bull on the third dart is a home run. No explaining needed.
  • SteeplechaseRun from 20 round to 5 along the inner singles, clearing four hurdles that only a triple will jump, and finish on the bull.

Versus games

6 games
  • Green vs Red (2 Players)Green against red, one working clockwise and the other anti-clockwise. Only doubles and triples score, and hitting your opponent's number costs you points.
  • EliminationBeat your opponent's three-dart total every round. Lose or tie and a life goes; three lives and you are out.
  • Warfare (2 Players)The board splits into two armies of ten numbers. Wipe out your opponent's soldiers to win - and mind you don't shoot your own.
  • Advanced Warfare (2 Players)Warfare with minefields: hit your opponent's doubles or triples and you lose one of your own soldiers instead. Rushing gets expensive.
  • Paintball (2 Players)A second route to victory in Warfare: three hits on the inner bull captures the enemy flag, so you never have to clear the whole board.
  • Fox Hunt (2 Players)One player is the mouse running for its hole, the other is the cat closing in. Get home and you win - the tension sits in the gap between them.

Casino games

3 games
  • Casino A - FlushStake 10 to 90 points each round and cash in only by hitting the called number, with doubles and triples multiplying the pot. Nerve pays.
  • Casino B - StraightCasino A with an extra layer: your three darts must work through three different called segments in order. Bigger stakes, longer odds.
  • Casino C - Three StarOnly 15-20 and the bull are live, and it takes three hits in one turn to collect at triple your stake. The hardest of the three Casino games.
